The rules

Always

  • Someone watches every time you do a skill
  • Send people to this playbook before you answer it yourself
  • Three-way your recruit's first calls, then step back
  • Recognize wins in front of the team

Never

  • Do something for a recruit that you haven't shown them
  • Let a new person go a day without a Day 1 and a Fast Start School date
  • Carry someone who isn't making their 2 calls
  • Skip a 30-day checkpoint

Sunday review

Ten minutes on your own. Update your clocks first, then answer honestly and send it to your leader.
