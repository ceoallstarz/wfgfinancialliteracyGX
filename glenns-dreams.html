The Synthesis

One mission, six needs, all pointing the same way

Glenn's top two are Contribution and Significance, held together by Connection. That combination is rare and it's a gift — most high-significance people burn out or step on others to feed the need. His only gets satisfied when other families win.

That's the deep reason the mission and the money never compete for Glenn. For most people, ambition and generosity pull in opposite directions. For him, they're the same motion.

The one honest caution: because Contribution and Significance both run so hot, the risk isn't laziness — it's overextension. Saying yes to everything, carrying the team on Glenn's hours, going back to work and sprinting the baseshop. Certainty — rest, systems, insurance, faith — is what keeps the other five from burning him down. Protect it on purpose.
