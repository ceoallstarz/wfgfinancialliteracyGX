Mojave Mayhem

8 stages of rifle and pistol in the Barstow desert. Fastest adjusted time takes 100 on each stage.

Up to 7 shooters. Scores stay on this phone.

How scoring works

Adjusted time is raw time plus penalties. On each stage, the fastest adjusted time earns 100 points. Everyone else earns 100 × (best time ÷ their time). Highest total points wins the match, so a long stage counts the same as a short one.

Steel miss, or paper miss+2.0
Paper C-zone hit+0.5
Paper D-zone hit+1.0
Procedural (wrong order, safety off while moving, wrong position)+3.0
Skipped target+2 per required hit

Head shots count only in the head box. A body shot on a head-required target scores as a miss. Steel in the Steel Sprint is hit-to-continue, so only its paper takes penalties.

Range layout

Four targets stay put all day. Shooters move between four cones instead.

  • Paper A and Paper B: side by side on the paper line, about 2 yards apart.
  • Steel 2 (pistol only): 5 yards past the paper line, off to the left.
  • Steel 1 (rifle and long pistol): 40 yards past the paper line, center-right.
  • Cone 1: 10 yards from the paper. That’s 15 yards to Steel 2 and 50 to Steel 1.
  • Cone 2: 15 yards from the paper, straight back from Cone 1.
  • Cone 3: 25 yards from the paper, the sprint start.
  • Cone 4: 10 yards from the paper, 10 yards left of Cone 1.

Paste both papers after every shooter. Bring plenty of pasters and a spare set of paper.

Safety is the whole game

  • One shooter at a time. A range officer runs the timer and walks behind the shooter.
  • The muzzle never breaks the 180 toward the line. Turn drills start facing uprange with the pistol holstered, or the rifle staged on a barrel, muzzle downrange.
  • Finger off the trigger while moving, reloading, or clearing a malfunction. Rifle safety goes on before every movement and every transition.
  • Rifles only ever shoot Steel 1, at 50 yards or more. Steel 2 is pistol only, never rifle. AR500 or better, no dents or craters. No steel-core or green-tip ammo.
  • Disqualified for the day: negligent discharge, breaking the 180, sweeping anyone, or dropping a loaded gun.
  • Eyes and ears on everyone. A trauma kit is on the table, and someone knows where it is.

Desert Duel (optional finale)

Set two identical steel plates side by side, with a stop plate between them. Seed the bracket from the final leaderboard. Two shooters go at once from low ready. Clear your plates, then hit the stop plate first to win the round. Best of three. The top seeds get byes with 5 or 6 shooters. The winner takes the Duel title, separate from the match champ.

Round count per shooter

Scored runs need about 15 rifle and 37 or more pistol rounds. Bring 30 rifle and 75 pistol for a cushion and a practice run.
